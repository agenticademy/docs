## Build agents

For developers: a first agent in LangGraph.js, then systems of agents, then long and complex runs.

1. [Build your first agents](first-agents/README.md): From one model call to a guarded agent, with LangChain.js and LangGraph.js, in your browser. (Beginner · For developers · 8 chapters)
2. [Agent systems and standards](agent-systems/README.md): MCP, A2A, AG-UI and friends, orchestration, context, tracing and testing, with the real protocol code. (Intermediate · For developers · 8 chapters)
3. [Advanced agents](advanced-agents/README.md): State at scale, Command and Send, subgraphs, time travel, loops that survive and loops that stop, planning, deep agents, and jobs that span sessions. (Advanced · For developers · 10 chapters)
